Cookie policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

bagscope.org does not use cookies.

Cookies: none

Local storage, session storage or other browser storage: none

Tracking pixels, analytics or advertising tags: none

Third-party content that could set its own cookies: none. Fonts and scripts are stored on bagscope.org itself.

Why there is no cookie banner

EU law (Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive, as implemented in Belgian law) requires consent before a website stores or reads information on your device, unless it is strictly necessary. This website stores and reads nothing on your device, so there is nothing to ask consent for, and a banner would only get in your way.

The server responses from GitHub Pages, which hosts the site, also set no cookies. Your browser may keep its own normal cache of the pages to load them faster. That cache is controlled by your browser, not by this site.

The Bagscope app

The desktop app is not a website and uses no tracking cookies. It keeps its data in its own folder on your PC. See the privacy policy.

If this changes

If bagscope.org ever adds anything that needs consent, this page will be updated first and the site will ask for your consent before anything is stored.
