For people with more coins than they can keep in their head

Phantom shows one account at a time and happily values an illiquid airdrop at a fantasy price. Bagscope answers the questions that matter.

What am I worth?

A total you can trust

Balances come straight from the Solana network for every wallet you add, priced from the pool with the deepest liquidity.

  • Coins with no real price, or more value than could ever be sold, are left out, and each says why
  • 24h, 7d, 30d, 6-month and 1-year performance
  • If data fails to load, it says so. It never fills a gap with a guess
Is this about to rug?

A risk score that shows its working

Low, Medium or High, built from six checks you can read one by one.

  • Liquidity, top-10 holder share, token age
  • Mint and freeze authority, LP locked or burned
  • Whether the developer wallet is holding, reduced or sold
Is this the real one?

Catches fake coins with the same name

When two coins in your wallets share a ticker, Bagscope warns you and compares them side by side by liquidity and risk.

Am I actually doing well?

Your bags against just holding something else

Your holdings next to SOL, Bitcoin, Ethereum, a meme coin index, and the S&P 500, Nasdaq 100 and gold through tokenised funds.

  • Entries fill themselves in from your transaction history
  • Average entry, realized and unrealized profit per coin
Tell me when it moves

Alerts as Windows notifications

Type amounts the way you say them: 5m, 250k, 1.2b.

  • Price or market cap above or below
  • Whale trades over a size you choose
  • Liquidity being pulled from the main pool

Questions people ask first

Can Bagscope move or spend my coins?

No. It has no swap, send or signing feature for transactions. Connecting a wallet only shares its public address, the same information anyone can already see on a block explorer.

Why does it open my browser to connect?

Phantom is a browser extension, so the approval has to happen where Phantom lives. Bagscope opens a small page on your own computer, Phantom asks you to approve, and only the public address comes back.

Where do the numbers come from?

Balances and transactions from the Solana network. Prices from Jupiter, with DexScreener as a fallback. Risk data from RugCheck and Jupiter. Each figure in the app says where it came from and when it was last updated.

How accurate is the performance history?

Balances and current prices are exact. Charts for dates before you installed the app value today's holdings at past prices, and the app labels them as an estimate. Profit and loss per coin comes from your real swaps.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not yet. Bagscope runs on Windows 10 and 11.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no token. If it is useful to you, there is an optional tip address below.
