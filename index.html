Every Phantom wallet. One real number.

Bagscope is a free Windows app for Solana meme coin holders. It adds up all your wallets from on-chain balances, works out what you actually paid, and shows the warning signs before a coin rugs. It is view-only and can never move your funds.

Built for people who hold more coins than they can keep in their head

Phantom shows one account at a time and happily values an illiquid airdrop at a fantasy price. Bagscope answers the four questions that matter.

What am I worth?

A total you can trust

Balances are read straight from the Solana network for every wallet you add, then priced from the pool with the deepest liquidity.

  • Coins with no real price, or more value than could ever be sold, are left out and each one says why
  • 24h, 7d, 30d, 6-month and 1-year performance
  • All wallets together, or one at a time, with your own labels
  • If data fails to load, it says so. It never fills a gap with a guess
Is this about to rug?

A risk score that shows its working

Low, Medium or High, built from six checks you can read one by one instead of a mystery number.

  • Liquidity, top-10 holder share, token age
  • Mint and freeze authority, LP locked or burned
  • Whether the developer wallet is holding, reduced or sold
  • Flags from RugCheck listed underneath
Is this the real one?

Catches fake coins with the same name

Anyone can launch a token with any ticker. When two coins in your wallets share a name, Bagscope puts a warning on the row and lets you compare them side by side by liquidity and risk.

  • Also searches for other coins using that ticker
  • Verified coins are marked
Am I actually doing well?

Your bags against just holding something else

One chart, everything starting from zero: your holdings next to SOL, Bitcoin, Ethereum, a meme coin index, and the S&P 500, Nasdaq 100 and gold through tokenised funds.

  • Entries fill themselves in: Bagscope reads your transaction history and records every swap at the price you paid
  • Average entry, realized and unrealized profit per coin
  • Daily snapshots and CSV export
Tell me when it moves

Alerts as Windows notifications

Set a level once and get on with your day. Type amounts the way you say them: 5m, 250k, 1.2b.

  • Price above or below
  • Market cap above or below
  • Whale trades over a size you choose
  • Liquidity being pulled from the main pool

Install it in a minute

The same three steps whether your wallet lives in a browser extension or on your phone.

  1. Download and run the installerChoose the folder and whether you want a desktop shortcut.
  2. Add a walletConnect Phantom on this PC, scan a QR code with your phone, or paste an address to watch.
  3. Approve and sign a short messageThat proves the address is yours. It is not a transaction and costs nothing. Bagscope keeps only the public address.

Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” because the installer is new and not yet signed by a certificate. Choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”. On PCs with Smart App Control switched on, Windows may refuse unsigned apps altogether.

Questions people ask first

Can Bagscope move or spend my coins?

No. It has no swap, send or signing feature for transactions. Connecting a wallet only shares its public address, the same information anyone can already see on a block explorer.

Why does it open my browser to connect?

Phantom is a browser extension, so the approval has to happen where Phantom lives. Bagscope opens a small page on your own computer, Phantom asks you to approve, and only the public address comes back.

Where do the numbers come from?

Balances and transactions from the Solana network. Prices from Jupiter, with DexScreener as a fallback. Risk data from RugCheck and Jupiter. The stock index and gold benchmarks follow tokenised funds on Solana. Each figure in the app says where it came from and when it was last updated.

How accurate is the performance history?

Balances and current prices are exact. Charts for dates before you installed the app value today's holdings at past prices, and the app labels them as an estimate. Profit and loss per coin comes from your real swaps.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not yet. Bagscope currently runs on Windows 10 and 11.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no token. If it is useful to you, there is an optional tip address below.

Support the dev

Bagscope is free. If it saves you time or a rug, a tip in SOL keeps it going. Send from your own wallet on the Solana network; the app has the same address with a QR code under “Support the dev”.
