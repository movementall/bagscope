Accessibility

Last updated 14 October 2026

I want bagscope.org to be usable by everyone, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard only, high zoom or reduced motion.

What has been done

  • The aim is the WCAG 2.1 level AA guidelines.
  • Every text and background colour combination was measured and meets the AA contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1. The lowest on the site is 5.5 to 1.
  • Every screenshot has a text description (alt text). Decorative drawings are hidden from screen readers or described in words next to them.
  • The whole site works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline, and a "Skip to content" link at the top.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, animations and smooth scrolling are switched off and everything is shown in its final state.
  • The moving example dashboard at the top has a Pause button.
  • The page language is set, headings are in a logical order, and the layout reflows to a single column on small screens, with a menu button on phones.

Known limitations

  • The chart that draws itself while you scroll is a picture. The same information is given as text in the list next to it.
  • Small text inside the app screenshots can be hard to read. The important points are written in the text beside each screenshot.

Report a problem

If something on this site does not work for you, please email artem.shvetts@gmail.com and describe what happened. I will try to fix it or give you the information another way.

This statement is voluntary. As a free, non-commercial hobby project, bagscope.org is not covered by the European Accessibility Act.
