Donations and refunds

Last updated 14 October 2026

Bagscope is free. There is nothing to buy: no subscription, no paid version and no in-app purchases. So there are no purchases to refund.

Donations

You can support the project by sending SOL to the address shown on the home page and in the app under "Support the dev":

3iVACqzJpf2cKU3UD6fA4ca2diSATs8J9qvozV9QD7f5

  • A donation is a voluntary gift. You do not receive any product, service, feature, priority support or other benefit in return.
  • Bagscope is not a charity, so donations are not tax-deductible.
  • Only send from a wallet you control, on the Solana network. Check the address carefully: it is shown in full so you can compare it.
  • Never send anyone your seed phrase. Donating never requires it.

Refunds

Blockchain transfers cannot be reversed by anyone, and I cannot take money back from your wallet. Because a donation is a gift, there is no right to a refund.

If you sent a donation by mistake, for example the wrong amount, email artem.shvetts@gmail.com within 14 days with the transaction ID. I will look at it in good faith and, where reasonable, send the amount back to the sending address minus the network fee. I cannot promise this in every case.

Scams

I will never contact you first to ask for money, offer a "refund", or ask you to connect a wallet to a website. Anyone who does is not me.
