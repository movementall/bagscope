Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Short version: this website collects nothing about you itself. It uses no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it loads no files from other companies. The Bagscope app stores everything on your own PC and sends nothing to its developer.

Who is responsible

The controller for this website is Artem Shvets, a private individual in Belgium, reachable at artem.shvetts@gmail.com. There is no data protection officer, because none is required for a project like this.

What happens when you visit this website

Hosting by GitHub

bagscope.org is served by GitHub Pages. To deliver the pages and protect the service, GitHub receives technical data from every visit, including your IP address, the page requested, the time and your browser's user agent. GitHub may keep this in its server logs for security purposes. I do not receive or see these logs and I cannot link them to you.

GitHub, Inc. is in the United States. It describes how it handles this data, and the safeguards it uses for transfers from the EU, in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in making the website available and secure. A website cannot be delivered without these technical requests.

No cookies, analytics or third-party content

  • No cookies and no local storage are used. See the cookie policy.
  • No analytics, statistics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • No embedded content from other companies: no videos, maps, social media buttons or chat widgets.
  • Fonts and scripts are stored on bagscope.org itself, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts or any other content network.
  • There are no forms on this website and no account to create.

Downloading the app

The installer is downloaded from bagscope.org itself, so only GitHub's hosting logs described above are involved.

Links to other websites

Links such as "Source code" lead to GitHub.com. Once you follow a link, the privacy policy of that website applies.

Wallet links between your PCs

The app can create a link that moves your wallet list to another PC. The wallet addresses sit in the part of the link after "#". Browsers never send that part to a website, so bagscope.org/add only passes it to the Bagscope app on your own PC. It is not sent to or stored by me.

If you email me

If you write to artem.shvetts@gmail.com, I receive your email address, your name if you include it, and your message. I use them only to answer you. Legal basis: legitimate interest in replying to your message (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). I delete the conversation when it is no longer needed, and at the latest 12 months after it ends, unless the law requires me to keep it. The email service is Gmail, run by Google, which processes the email under its own privacy policy.

If you send a donation

Donations are made on the Solana blockchain. Your sending wallet address, the amount and the time are public on the blockchain for everyone and cannot be deleted by anyone, including me. I do not try to find out who is behind a sending address. See donations and refunds.

The Bagscope app

The app runs on your Windows PC. It has no account, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no analytics. The developer receives no data from it.

Stored only on your PC

  • The public wallet addresses you add, with their names and colours.
  • Settings, alerts, watchlist, notes and entries, and a cache of prices and transactions.
  • Optional keys you enter yourself (a custom RPC address, an xAI key, X API access). These are encrypted with Windows' built-in protection for your user account.

To delete all of it, remove your wallets in the app and uninstall it, or delete the folder %APPDATA%\Bagscope.

Services the app contacts from your PC

To show balances, prices and risk data, the app sends requests straight from your PC to public data services. Each of them receives your IP address and the wallet or token addresses being looked up, and handles them under its own privacy policy. I am not involved in these requests.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. Because I hold almost no data about visitors, most requests will concern emails you sent me. Write to artem.shvetts@gmail.com. I will answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels, www.dataprotectionauthority.be, or to the authority in your own EU country.

Children

Bagscope is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date. If the website ever starts using cookies or collecting data, that will only happen after the policy says so and, where the law requires it, after you have agreed.
